# Judge prompt · style C (live product lift)

You are judging a vertical 9:16 reel that promotes a live web page of interactive demos. Verify with ffmpeg yourself (1 fps
sheet, frames at every cut, `ebur128`, `volumedetect`); don't trust the description. Client decisions you must not penalise: the
speaker's own take in the lower half, bold colour fields, one font family, intro and outro stings, an official mascot asset.

Score 0–100 with these weights, then give at most 8 fixes ranked by impact, each with its time and the exact change:
1. **Face (20)** — no demo, chip, card or caption overlaps the face on any frame; lip sync holds across any take join.
2. **Demos on the words (20)** — each named example appears on its word and its key moment lands on the spoken payoff; the lift
   reads as rising out of the page, not as a pop-in.
3. **Captions (15)** — under the chin, the spoken word lit, inside the platform safe area, no stale pill.
4. **Sharpness (15)** — demo UI text readable at phone size, no blocking, no upscaled captures.
5. **Rhythm (15)** — cuts on the beat grid, no dead stretch over ~2 s, the hook lands in 3 s.
6. **Sound (10)** — voice −14 ±0.7 LUFS, music clearly under the voice, every SFX tied to a visual.
7. **CTA (5)** — keyword readable for ≥ 2 s, wording right for the network.
